📶 USA Free WiFi Tracker

This is the no-script fallback for old phones, slow connections, or browsers with JavaScript disabled. It can't run the live search the full app uses — see why below — but it still gets you to a working answer.

Why there's no offline spot list here

This tracker's sibling ISP-quality trackers can ship a static, no-JS provider table because each one tracks a small, fixed list of licensed ISPs per country. Free WiFi doesn't work that way — there's no fixed list; it's whatever's near your specific address right now, pulled live from OpenStreetMap. A pre-built offline list would either be empty for most of the country or stale within days. Honest tradeoff: the full app needs JavaScript to do that live lookup.

What you can do right now

1. If your phone can open a link at all, open the full USA Free WiFi Tracker — it needs JavaScript but nothing else (no account, no install).

2. If you're on a very old device or truly offline, search your mobile carrier's own "free WiFi near me" feature, or ask at any public library — nearly every US public library system offers free WiFi during open hours.
